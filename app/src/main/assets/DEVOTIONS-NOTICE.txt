Devotional resources / 读经、玫瑰经与祷词

The traditional prayers (Sign of the Cross, Apostles' Creed, Our Father,
Hail Mary, Glory Be, Hail Holy Queen, Rosary concluding prayer, Act of
Contrition and Grace before Meals) have long-established public-domain
origins. The Fatima invocation dates to 1917. Traditional English forms
are used. No modern Bible translation passages are reproduced here.

Chinese prayers are project translations/compilations of these traditional
prayers, not a claim to reproduce an approved regional liturgical edition.
Morning and evening prayers, mystery meditations, explanatory guidance,
the data arrangement and Chinese translations are project authored and
distributed under the project's MIT license. Simplified Chinese is the
authored source; Traditional Chinese is generated using opencc-js, with
the same edition identity. Changing the interface language selects these
devotional translations; it does not change the original classic corpora.

The Holy See Rosary pages were consulted for mystery group names, weekday
suggestions, scriptural references and the Our Father / ten Hail Marys /
Glory Be structure of each decade. No Vatican photographs, modern scripture
quotations or page commentary are copied into the application. The guide
uses the common Apostles' Creed opening; customs can vary. The weekday
suggestion is not presented as a mandatory liturgical calendar.
